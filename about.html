About me

The person behind the parrot

Noah Peter

Hi, I'm Noah, the founder of Purple Parrot Games. I build apps and games for iPhone and iPad, with a soft spot for thoughtful design, smooth animation and systems that are fun to dig into.

Most of my work is written in Swift using SwiftUI and SpriteKit. I care about the details: clean architecture, careful typography and interfaces that feel at home on Apple devices.

Right now I'm working on my first big title, Fashion Inc. More projects are coming soon.
